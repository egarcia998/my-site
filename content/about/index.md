I am a Uruguayan mathematician currently completing a joint PhD between 
LAAS-CNRS / Université de Toulouse III (France) and PEDECIBA / Universidad de la 
República (Uruguay), under the supervision of <a href="https://www.fing.edu.uy/~paola/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Paola Bermolen</a> and <a href="https://www.laas.fr/en/annuaire/463" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Matthieu Jonckheere</a>.

My doctoral project focuses on reinforcement learning in sparse reward environments, using tools from rare event simulation and stochastic processes.
