My research lies in applied probability, with a focus on reinforcement learning (RL)
algorithms in hard-exploration environments — settings where reward signals are sparse
or highly stochastic. I study these problems through the framework of rare event
estimation and simulation, using tools from stochastic processes, measure changes via
exponential martingales, large deviation principles, and Feynman–Kac semigroups.

### Research projects

- **Stochastic Models for Complex Interacting Systems** · MATH-AmSud (South America–France), 2025–2027.  
  Analysis of complex stochastic systems: interacting particle systems, random graphs, log-correlated fields, and SDEs. Role: Participant.

- **Learning and Control on Complex Networks (LAGOON)** · STIC-AmSud (Argentina–Uruguay–France), 2023–2025.  
  Machine learning and probability on complex networks. Coordinated by Prof. Matthieu Jonckheere (France). Role: Participant.

- **Grupo CSIC Probabilidad y Estadística** · CSIC, Universidad de la República (Uruguay), 2023–.  
  Coordinators: Prof. Ernesto Mordecki and Prof. Federico Dalmao.

- **Latitude – Crossroad between dynamical systems and group actions** · MATH-AmSud (Uruguay–France–Chile), 2020–2022.  
  Dynamics of flows in negative curvature, entropy, Lyapunov exponents, and growth in discrete groups of isometries. Included a research stay at Université de Rennes 1 (November 2021), under the supervision of Prof. Françoise Dal'Bo. Role: Participant.
