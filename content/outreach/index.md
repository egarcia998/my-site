**Imaginary Itinerante** · Facultad de Ciencias, Universidad de la República · August 2016 – October 2017  
A mathematical outreach project visiting 14 cities across Uruguay, presenting interactive
mathematical content to the general public. The project was documented in a short film.  
[imaginary.org/project/imaginary-uruguay](https://www.imaginary.org/es/project/imaginary-uruguay)

**Latitud Ciencias** · Facultad de Ciencias, Universidad de la República · September 2016  
Annual science outreach event open to school groups, families, and the general public.
Staffed the mathematics stand, presenting research and interactive mathematical content.

**Programa de Visitas a Facultad de Ciencias** · Universidad de la República · October–November 2019  
Organised workshops and recreational mathematics activities for primary and secondary school
groups visiting the Faculty of Sciences (four sessions with schools from Tarariras, Toledo, and Montevideo).
