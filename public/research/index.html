Ernesto García Ciganda

Research

My research lies in applied probability, with a focus on reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms in hard-exploration environments — settings where reward signals are sparse or highly stochastic. I study these problems through the framework of rare event estimation and simulation, using tools from stochastic processes, measure changes via exponential martingales, large deviation principles, and Feynman–Kac semigroups.
