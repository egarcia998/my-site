Ernesto García Ciganda

About

I am a Uruguayan mathematician currently completing a joint PhD between LAAS-CNRS / Université de Toulouse III (France) and PEDECIBA / Universidad de la República (Uruguay), under the supervision of Paola Bermolen and Matthieu Jonckheere.

My doctoral project focuses on reinforcement learning in sparse reward environments, using tools from rare event simulation and stochastic processes.
