Research supervision

Research intern co-supervision · LAAS-CNRS / Toulouse INP, April–June 2024
Co-supervised with Dr. Matthieu Jonckheere. Over seven weeks, guided Malik Hacini in a research project on generalized spectral clustering. The internship resulted in an open-source repository (github.com/Malik-Hacini/Generalized-Spectral-Clustering ) and a written monograph presented at Toulouse INP.
